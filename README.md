# kelly-james
## Christmas card (`christmas/`)
An animated Christmas card: a red envelope on a candlelit Christmas table, tap to open, and the photo card rises out.
Personalise it with the card maker (`christmas/maker.html`). The table is decorated with hand-drawn art drawn in code:
a pine branch with baubles, a flickering candle with holly, a wrapped present and pine cones. The envelope has faint
embossed snowflakes and the card has gold corner flourishes.

## Birthday card (`birthday/`)

Same envelope opening as the Christmas card, on a party table (bunting, balloons, a present, drifting confetti).
Inside is a hand-drawn cake with five lit candles: tap **Blow out the candles**, allow the microphone and blow
on the phone, and the flames lean and go out one by one (or just tap the flames). Then you get smoke, confetti
and a music-box "Happy Birthday", plus a WhatsApp thank-you button.

Personalise with the link: `birthday/?to=Tom&from=James%20%26%20Kelly`. Optional: `&msg=…`, `&greeting=…`,
`&reply=447…` (the WhatsApp number the thank-you goes to). Short links with their own preview picture live
in sub-folders, e.g. `birthday/tom/`.
